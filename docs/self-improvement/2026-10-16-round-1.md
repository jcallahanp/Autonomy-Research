# Self-improvement round 1: results

Date: 2026-10-16. Series 1, round 1 of 2. Base: v0.1 merge commit 981a205.
Plan: `2026-10-16-round-1-plan.md`. Implementer and review record: `round-1-implementer-report.md`.

## What the round did

1. **Measured v0.1** on the frozen exam: 8 offline cases and 2 live web cases, 3 runs each,
   each run paired with a no-plugin baseline.
2. **Researched itself** with its own research skill: 5 researcher workers (one audited the
   plugin's own files), one synthesizer, one verifier. Findings:
   `research/findings/research-agent-self-improvement.md`.
3. **Checked that research twice.** The verifier sampled 20 claims and verified 19. An
   independent hand check of 20 other claims found 17 supported, 3 partly supported and none
   wrong (`research/runs/research-agent-self-improvement/hand-check.md`). Two of the three
   partial ones were introduced when the synthesizer merged notes, which is what the outside
   studies predicted and what change 2 targets.
4. **Locked the exam** with a hash manifest before changing anything.
5. **Applied 10 changes** from the findings, then one review on the most capable model
   (0 critical, 7 important, all fixed).
6. **Re-measured, traced every dip, and fixed what the traces showed** (three more fixes,
   below). Then measured again on the final commit.
7. **Ran the improved verifier on the five earlier findings** that had never been verified.

The keep rule held: the frozen exam did not drop, and every change has a stated reason in the
plan. With 8 cases x 3 runs, only changes of about 0.2-0.3 are distinguishable from noise
(findings, eval-without-overfit facet), so the 0.94 to 0.98 rise is not by itself proof of
improvement. The stronger evidence is the traced failures below, each found in a transcript and
each now covered by a check.

`architecture` and `methodology` are now complete; `context-management`, `packaging` and
`tool-routing` are needs-review (small number mismatches, a dropped scope, one figure cited to
the wrong page).

## Failures the re-measure found, and what fixed them

| Symptom | Cause found in the transcript | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cite-or-park lost its note in 2 of 5 runs (and 1 of 3 in v0.1) | The researcher resolved its relative note path against the plugin folder it read its schema from, so the note was written outside the project | The lead gives absolute paths under the project root; the researcher resolves relative paths against the working directory |
| contradiction's citation judge failed 2 of 3 runs (1 of 3 in v0.1) | After Evidence became one bullet per claim, answers cited only Evidence numbers, and an inferred qualifier borrowed a URL | Summary and answers cite URLs inline; an unquoted qualifier is labelled a worker inference |
| triage-real-repo skipped its registry row in 4 of 9 runs | The lead found no `research/tool-registry.md` and wrote nothing | Triage and tool scan create the registry with its header |
| quote-carry's evidence-has-quote failed every run | A grader defect: in the 2 runs kept for inspection the quotes were there, in a numbered list the grader did not accept | Grader accepts numbered lists; a fixture from a real run pins it |

## Measurement notes

- The live cases need the sandbox backend installed (`apt install bubblewrap socat`) and the
  self-test skill's full `--allow-tools` list. Without the list, the web tools are silently
  withheld and the live score drops to 0.5. The self-improve skill now carries the full command.
- Raw results are git-ignored under `evals/results/round1-*.json`.
