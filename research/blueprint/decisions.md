## Other tensions decided here
- Docker on the VM: feasibility wins over runtime's "no Docker is required" (engineer-runtime P1): trials need a container because the Bash sandbox "is not sufficient for fully unattended runs" (engineer-feasibility C16). Bootstrap adds Docker or rootless Podman.
- Process shape: per-task `claude -p` under a supervisor (engineer-context §Q7.4; engineer-human-loop E126, E129) wins over runtime's always-open session (engineer-runtime §Q7), because Channels in `-p` disable interactive tools (engineer-human-loop E115) and permission prompts stay open until answered (E109; other forwarded dialogs expire after five minutes to a no-action default). A daytime interactive session stays for Channels and Remote Control.
- Permission mode: `--permission-mode auto --permission-prompts none` plus sandbox and managed deny rules (engineer-human-loop E126, E129, E148) over runtime's skip-permissions (engineer-runtime S7), because security forbids bypass where untrusted input is read (engineer-security E113) and bypass on the host was rejected (engineer-human-loop E133, E134).
- Approval tier names: one scheme. T0 automatic, T1 digest line, T2 hard gate (engineer-human-loop §Q6). Protocol's merge Tier 1 is T0 only while R16 is on, otherwise a T1 "merge PR?" line; protocol's Tier 2 and `NEEDS-JOHN` are T1 lines unless they hit a hard gate (engineer-protocol §Q5).
- Human-loop's T2 list also named external mail and secrets; under ruling 9 they are T1 by default and T2 only where a profile says so.
- Brief size: three pages (engineer-briefing §Q2, John's cap) replaces planning's 1-2 pages (engineer-planning §Q1).
- Approval log: `decisions/approvals.jsonl` (engineer-human-loop §Q6) is the one log; protocol's `decisions.json` poller output (engineer-protocol §Q6, no source) writes into it.
- One push channel: the supervisor's Telegram bot; Routines' Slack digest (engineer-runtime C49) is not used, so the batch stays on one channel (requirements Constraints). ntfy is the alternative (engineer-protocol E102).
- Cross-family review scope: both limits apply, Tier 2 changes (protocol) and only `public` or `internal` data (engineer-models §Q8).
- Path cap for trials: 3 by default, configurable (engineer-feasibility §Q3 Contradictions).
- Dead-man route: healthchecks.io Hobbyist, free for 20 jobs (engineer-deploy E131), notifying John's phone by SMS where the tier offers it (ruling 3), else by its Telegram or email integration (untested, SP72); Twilio SMS at $0.0083/segment (engineer-human-loop E121) is the alternative. Reason: the check is external, so it still fires when the VM or supervisor is dead, which a ping sent from the VM cannot do.
